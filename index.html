    How to write a song 
    title: My Song
tempo: 80
time: 4/4
Do4 q, Re4 q, Mi4 h |
Fa#4 q, Sol4 q, La4 h |
    • Note = name + octave: Do4 Fa#4 Sib4 (or C4 F#4 Bb4). 4 = middle-C octave.
    • Length after it: w whole, h half, q quarter, e eighth, s 16th; add . for dotted (h.). No length = q.
    • | = bar line, R = rest (R h).
    • Finger (optional): / + 1–5 after the note: Si4/5 q (1 = thumb, 5 = pinky). Left hand: Do3/5L.
    • Optional lines: key: G, clef: bass, look: open (round notes without stems), LH: line for a left hand.
